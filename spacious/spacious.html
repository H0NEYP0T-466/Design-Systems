Foundational Design Intent

Spacious prioritizes structure, scanability, and predictable vertical rhythm. No decorative noise, no off-palette distractions.

Structure

8pt Baseline Grid

Every vertical margin, internal card padding, and component gap steps cleanly in multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96px. No ad-hoc offsets.

margin-bottom: var(--space-32);
Whitespace

Air Before Borders

Separate distinct concerns through generous spatial intervals rather than thick borders or heavy drop shadows. Let the interface breathe.

padding: var(--space-48);
Hierarchy

Explicit Action Stance

Primary actions consistently command #3B82F6, while secondary controls remain neutral. Hierarchy is immediate and unmistakable.

background: #3B82F6;
