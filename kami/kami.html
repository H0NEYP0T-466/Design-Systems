02

The Paper Discipline

kami is not a component library for buttons and modals; it is a typographical agreement between author and reader.

"When the surface is parchment and the ink is dark blue, the eye slows down. We read for comprehension rather than skimming for notification badges."

Traditional digital user interfaces treat typography as an afterthought wrapped in plastic shadows and colored pills. In kami, the serif typeface carries the entire architectural burden of hierarchy:

  • No Synthetic Bold: Headlines, section markers, and subtitles all share weight 500, varying only in size and vertical spacing.
  • No Italic: Emphasis is never slanted. If a sentence requires gravitas, it shifts to ink-blue or takes a solid tag fill.
  • Solid Tag Hex Fills: Print engines double-render alpha values; tag backgrounds must use solid hex values like #E4ECF5.
  • Whisper Shadows: Zero hard drop-shadows; elevation is communicated through a 1px ring and 4px blur whisper.
