Design Guidelines & Anti-Patterns

Strict rules for preserving Webex's disciplined collaboration aesthetic and accessibility standards.

✓ Webex Canonical Practice
  • Blue Action Clarity: Keep primary CTA buttons and active controls strictly in Webex Action Blue (#1170cf).
  • Multi-User Presence Accents: Use Team Cobalt, Mint, Orange, Pink, etc., exclusively for avatars, presence markers, and workspace identity chips.
  • Pill Geometry: Use border-radius: 9999px for interactive buttons and control chips; use 16px radius for containers and cards.
  • 44px Touch Affordance: Maintain at least 44px × 44px hit bounds for all call controls, tabs, and roster actions.
  • Light Canvas + Dark Embedded Surface: Blend clean white productivity canvases with charcoal dark-mode surfaces for in-meeting chrome.
✗ Prohibited Anti-Patterns
  • No Consumer Social Rainbow: Do not turn Webex into a consumer party app by splashing vibrant secondary gradients everywhere.
  • Never Spectrum as CTAs: Never use Team Orange, Lime, or Pink as primary action or "Join Meeting" button backgrounds.
  • Avoid Heavy Corporate Darkness: Do not darken every surface into corporate black when the artifact is intended to feel collaborative and open.
  • No Decorative Font Theatrics: Avoid whimsical display serif or ultra-bold weights that hinder scannability in dashboards.
  • No Loud Spring Physics: Keep transitions smooth, practical, and in the 160ms–280ms duration bracket.
