PHILOSOPHICAL TENETS

THE BRUTALIST CODE

Strict uncompromising axioms that guide every structural intervention and digital artifact created by Atelier Béton.

01

NO FACADE CLADDING

Never conceal the true load-bearing material. If the structure is poured concrete, the surface must show the grain of timber formwork and the marks of tie-rods.

02

EXPOSED MECHANICAL SYSTEMS

Air ducts, conduit paths, structural joints, and expansion gaps are not eyesores to be hidden; they are the vital organs of the building.

03

WEIGHT & MASS INTEGRITY

A structure must convey the visceral gravity of its mass. Thin veneer mimics of monumental weight are an insult to genuine architecture.
