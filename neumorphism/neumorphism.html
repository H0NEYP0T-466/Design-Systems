Rule Enforcement

Anti-Patterns (Section 9)

Strict adherence requirements defined in neumorphism/DESIGN.md.

✕ Prohibited Practices

  • No Off-Palette Colors: Do not add random accent gradients or unrelated colors outside the 8 declared tokens.
  • No Flat Hierarchy: Always modulate weights and sizes in Space Mono; do not collapse headings into uniform body sizes.
  • No Readability Reductions: Neumorphic bevels must preserve minimum WCAG contrast for body text against #E7E5E4.
  • No Metaphor Blending: Never mix glassmorphism backdrops or skeuomorphic textures into pure monochromatic neumorphism.
