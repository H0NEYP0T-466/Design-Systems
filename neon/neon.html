Engineering Specifications

Anti-Patterns & Token Strictness

Rules enforced strictly from Section 9 of neon/DESIGN.md.

✕ Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

  • ✕ No Off-Palette Colors: Do not introduce random hex colors when Primary (#BBF351), Secondary (#00BCFF), Surface, or Text can resolve it.
  • ✕ No Hierarchy Flattening: Never use the same type size or weight across headlines and body copy.
  • ✕ No Obtrusive Effects: Glow effects must enhance, never diminish contrast or legibility (>14:1 text contrast preserved).
  • ✕ No Mixed Metaphors: Keep strict adherence to high-contrast electric morphism without blending unrelated motifs.
