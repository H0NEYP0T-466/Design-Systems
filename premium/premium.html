Section 05 // Specification Compliance

Design System Anti-Patterns & Principles

Directly derived from Section 9 of the official DESIGN.md specification.

Authentic Principles (Do)
  • Favor Primary #3B82F6: Keep CTA emphasis distinct and singular per viewport hierarchy.
  • Preserve Surface #FFFFFF: Rely on whitespace and subtle hairline borders before adding heavy dropshadows.
  • Strict Typography Scale: Adhere strictly to 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 24 / 30 / 36px font sizing.
  • Predictable Spacing Grid: Align modules exclusively using 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32px increments.
  • Purposeful 150–250ms Motion: Transitions must be fast and stable using calibrated easing curves.
Explicit Anti-Patterns (Do Not)
  • No Off-Palette Colors: Do not introduce random blues, purples, or greys when an existing token can solve the requirement.
  • No Flattened Typography: Do not flatten hierarchy by using identical type size or weight across disparate levels.
  • No Decorative Clutter: Avoid ornate drop shadows, busy skeuomorphic textures, or low-contrast gradients.
  • No Metaphor Mixing: Do not combine unrelated visual metaphors or inconsistent border radii in the same interface.
  • No Missing Interaction States: Hover, focus-visible, active, and disabled states must never be omitted.
