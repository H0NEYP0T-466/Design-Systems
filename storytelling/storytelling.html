Chapter 05 // Specification Directives

Narrative Principles & Anti-Patterns

Directly derived from Section 9 of the official DESIGN.md specification for Storytelling.

Design System Directives (Do)
  • Favor Primary #3B82F6: Command narrative action with confident blue CTA emphasis.
  • Playful Display Typography: Let Abril Fatface headlines infuse warmth, delight, and literary weight.
  • Legible Inter Body Copy: Optimize scannability and contrast on Surface #FFFFFF with deep Text #111827.
  • Expressive Desktop-First Scale: Preserve clear vertical rhythm across 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 28 / 42 / 58px.
  • Stable 150–250ms Interactions: Ensure hover, focus-visible, and active feedback occur smoothly with stable easing.
Strict Anti-Patterns (Do Not)
  • No Off-Palette Drifting: Do not introduce random teals, pinks, or olives when #3B82F6 and #8B5CF6 fulfill the hierarchy.
  • No Flattened Type Hierarchy: Never equalize font sizes between story headlines, subtext, and captions.
  • No Decorative Gimmicks that Harm Readability: Avoid low-contrast background textures or unreadable glowing text.
  • No Metaphor Mixing: Do not drop sterile enterprise spreadsheets into playful narrative storytelling.
  • No Missing Focus States: Interactive elements must always exhibit explicit focus-visible rings.
