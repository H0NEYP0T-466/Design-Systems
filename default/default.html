Core components

Standard cards

White background, 1px neutral border, 12px radius, and 20px internal padding with zero decorative shadows.

Cobalt accent

One reserved accent (#2F6FEB) strictly used for primary calls to action, active indicators, and hyperlinks.

Semantic tokens

Success (#17A34A), Warning (#EAB308), and Danger (#DC2626) provide unambiguous status communication.

Interactive input discipline

Inputs feature 8px radius, 10px vertical padding, and clean border transitions without heavy drop shadows.

Elevation level 1 (Raised)

Reserved for floating overlays, active dropdowns, and modals: 2px y-offset, 8px blur, 8% opacity.
