$ xai-query --prompt "Explain the cosmological horizon problem and how cosmic inflation resolves it."
The cosmological horizon problem arises because standard Big Bang expansion cannot explain why regions of the CMB separated by more than ~1 degree have nearly identical temperatures (within 1 part in 100,000), even though in standard expansion they were causally disconnected at recombination. Cosmic inflation resolves this by postulating an exponential expansion phase driven by an inflaton scalar field in the early universe (t ~ 10^-36 s). This stretched a microscopic, causally connected patch into our entire observable universe, equalizing temperatures prior to the inflationary epoch.
