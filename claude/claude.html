Safety by Design · Chapter II

Frontier safety rooted in Constitutional principles.

Anthropic was founded on the conviction that as artificial intelligence grows more capable, alignment must deepen in tandem. Our research pioneers interpretability, evaluation, and safety guardrails.

Constitutional AI

Rather than relying solely on human feedback loops, models self-critique using a clear set of safety principles inspired by the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

Mechanistic Interpretability

Peering inside the neural network's weights to understand how Claude thinks, maps concepts, and avoids hallucinations before weights are deployed.

Enterprise Security

Your conversational data is never used to train our models. Fully compliant with SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR standards with zero data retention options.
