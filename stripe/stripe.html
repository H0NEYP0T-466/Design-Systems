Do's and Don'ts

Directly derived from Section 7 of the official Stripe design system specification.

Do

  • ✓ ss01 on all text: Always enable OpenType "ss01" for the geometric brand letterforms.
  • ✓ Weight 300 for headlines: Preserve whispered authority with light weight display titles.
  • ✓ Blue-tinted shadows: Use rgba(50,50,93,0.25) layered with neutral black shadows.
  • ✓ Deep Navy headings: Always use #061b31 instead of harsh pure black for text warmth.
  • ✓ Conservative border radius: Keep radii strictly within the 4px-8px range; never use pills on cards.

Don't

  • × No bold headline weights: Don't use weight 600-700 on display headlines; 300 is the brand voice.
  • × No pill-shaped buttons: Never use 9999px pills; buttons stay clean at 4px radius.
  • × No neutral gray shadows: Avoid generic gray drop shadows; always tint with blue (50,50,93).
  • × No pure black text: Never use #000000 for headings; always resolve to #061b31 deep navy.
  • × No decorative accent buttons: Do not use ruby or magenta for CTAs; purple (#533afd) is primary.
