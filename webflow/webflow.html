SYSTEM CONSTRAINTS

Webflow Do's and Don'ts

Preserve the authentic look and feel defined in open-design/design-systems/webflow/DESIGN.md.

✓ DO
  • Primary CTA Blue: Use Webflow Blue (#146ef5) for primary actions with hover state #0055d4.
  • Sharp 4px Radius: Keep functional buttons, cards, input controls, and badges tightly constrained to 4px–8px radius.
  • translate(6px) Motion: Apply the signature 6px translation hover animation to buttons and interactive links.
  • Uppercase Labels: Format badges and section categories in uppercase with 0.8px–1.5px letter-spacing.
  • 5-Layer Cascading Depth: Use multi-layer shadow stacks for authentic optical depth.
✗ DON'T
  • No Over-Rounding: Never round functional interactive elements beyond 8px (pills and bubbles violate Webflow geometry).
  • Never Secondary as CTA: Do not use purple, pink, green, orange, or yellow on primary CTA buttons.
  • No Generic Gray Shadows: Avoid single-value blurry shadows like 0 10px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.2).
  • No Pure Black Backgrounds: The Webflow canvas is crisp white (#ffffff) with near-black (#080808) text.
  • No Decorative Serif Mixing: Stick strictly to WF Visual Sans Variable and companion Inconsolata.
