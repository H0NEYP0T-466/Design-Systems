Section 05 // Specification Matrix

Design System Principles & Anti-Patterns

Directly derived from Section 9 of the official DESIGN.md specification for Professional.

Design System Directives (Do)
  • Favor Primary #FECE14: Command attention for the primary CTA using bold amber/gold contrast.
  • Authoritative Secondary #000000: Anchor headers, table titles, and secondary actions with high-contrast pure black.
  • Compact Mobile-First Typography: Scale cleanly across 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 22 / 28 / 34px using Poppins.
  • Consistent Vertical Cadence: Align modules exclusively with the 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32px spacing scale.
  • Crisp, Predictable Elevation: Use sharp hairline borders and structured offsets over blurry diffuse shadows.
Explicit Anti-Patterns (Do Not)
  • No Off-Palette Accents: Do not introduce random teals, purples, or blues when #FECE14 and #000000 solve the hierarchy.
  • No Low Contrast Text on Primary: Never place white text directly on #FECE14; always use dark text (#111827 or #000000) for legibility.
  • No Flattened Type Scale: Do not collapse headers and captions into uniform sizes.
  • No Decorative Gimmicks: Avoid ornate neon glows, floating badges without semantic value, or ambiguous iconography.
  • No Undefined Interaction States: Hover, focus-visible, active, and disabled states must be distinctly declared.
